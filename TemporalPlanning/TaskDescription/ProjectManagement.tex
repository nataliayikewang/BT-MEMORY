\subsubsection{Project Management}

Project management is essential for monitoring the project's progress and keeping the project on track.

\newpage
\textbf{PM1. Agile Ceremonies}

Run daily scrums, sprint plannings, sprint reviews, and sprint retrospectives. 

\textbf{PM2. Thesis Director Review Meetings}

Meet with the thesis director every two weeks to ensure the project is progressing correctly.

\textbf{PM3. Product Backlog Update}

Refine, update, and maintain the product backlog, which always reflects the current project status.
