\subsection{Temporal Planning}

Despite the project's overall progress being consistent with the initial plan, minor adjustments have been applied as expected. A small reordering of tasks was needed to keep progress moving forward.

The main change from the initial Gantt diagram is the postponement of the Dev3 task, due to the author's limited experience with pipeline development. A one-week dedication to this task revealed a shortfall compared to the original estimate of five days. Even though the effort exceeded the planned allocation, the task is still not completed. Given that it is not a high-priority requirement and that it has no strong dependencies on other tasks, Dev3 has been rescheduled to the final development stage. 

However, all other planned tasks have met their expected deadlines so far, and the delay of the Dev3 task does not have a significant impact on the project. The buffer time set aside for anticipated deviations is being used to complete this task and to prevent it from becoming a bottleneck in the project's execution.
