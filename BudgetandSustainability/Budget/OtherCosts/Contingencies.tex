\textbf{Contingencies}

No project strategy can eliminate the possibility of encountering obstacles. Therefore, a time buffer (27 hours) has been included to cover potential setbacks. However, simply adding extra time is insufficient, as it also entails additional resource consumption. 

For this reason, a portion of the project budget must be allocated for these potential scenarios. The proportional buffer time allocated to the total project duration is 5\% (27h/420).  Therefore, the contingency cost amounts to \textbf{€675.68}, which is the 5\% of the total general cost calculated so far: 

\[
PC\,(\text{€}) + GC\,(\text{€}) = 11{,}913.05 + 1{,}600.92 = \textbf{13{,}513.68\,\text{€}}
\]
