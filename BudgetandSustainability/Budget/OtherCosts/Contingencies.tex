
\textbf{Contingencies}
\vspace{-5pt}

No project strategy can eliminate the possibility of encountering obstacles. Therefore, a time buffer (27 hours) has been included to cover potential setbacks. However, simply adding extra time is insufficient, as it also entails additional resource consumption. 

For this reason, a portion of the project budget must be allocated for these potential scenarios. Therefore, the contingency cost amounts to \textbf{€1320.75}, which is the 10\% of the total general cost calculated so far: 

\vspace{-20pt}

\[
PC\,(\text{€}) + GC\,(\text{€}) = 11{,}913.05 + 1{,}294.40 = \textbf{13{,}207.45\,\text{€}}
\]
