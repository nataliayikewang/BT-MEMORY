\textbf{Contingencies}

No project strategy can eliminate the possibility of encountering obstacles. Therefore, a time buffer (27 hours) has been included to cover potential setbacks. However, simply adding extra time is insufficient, as it also entails additional resource consumption. Consequently, a portion of the project budget must be allocated for these potential scenarios. The proportional buffer time allocated to the total project duration is 5\% (27h/420).  Therefore, the contingency cost amounts to \textbf{€705.23}, which is the 5\% of the total general cost calculated so far (PC (€) + GC (€) = 13,370.03 + 1,600.92 = €14,970.94).
