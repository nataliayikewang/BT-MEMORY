Given that the Azure resource cost is \texteuro3.96 per month, the total cost for the project (122 days) is calculated as follows:

\vspace{-15pt}

\[
\text{Azure resource cost} = 
3.96 \text{ €/month} \times 
\dfrac{1 \text{ month}}{30 \text{ days}} \times 
122 \text{ days} = 
\textbf{\text{€}24.16}
\]

\vspace{-5pt}

Microsoft is also included in the software resources cost, demanding an annual fee of €99 \cite{microsoft-365-pricing}. This resource cost is calculated in the next equation:

\vspace{-15pt}

\[
\text{Microsoft resource cost} = 
99 \text{ €/year} \times 
\dfrac{1 \text{ year}}{365 \text{ days}} \times 
122 \text{ days} = 
\textbf{\text{€}33.09}
\]

\vspace{-5pt}

Thus, the total software resources cost equals the sum of both costs:

\begin{center}
    \texteuro24.16 + \texteuro33.09 = \textbf{\text{€}57.25}
\end{center}
