
\textbf{Software resources cost}
\vspace{-5pt}

Most software resources are open-source and are available for free. The primary expenses in this cost are Azure and Microsoft resources. 

Azure charges a pay-as-you-go fee and offers a customized price for each resource usage \cite{azure-pricing}. Since the application target user group is small, the most basic/free tier for each resource is considered. A monthly fee of €3.96 is estimated for this project, as shown in Table \ref{tab:azure-resource-cost}. 

\vspace{-17pt}


%Table of azure resources cost

    \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1}

    \footnotesize
    \begin{tabular}{@{}l c@{}}
     & \textbf{Cost} \\[-6pt]

    Blob Storage & 0.01 \\

    \hline

    \end{tabular}
    \caption[Azure resource cost]{Azure resource cost. [Own creation]}
    \label{tab:azure-resource-cost}
\end{table}


\vspace{-20pt}

Accordingly, the total Azure resources cost in the project is dervived:

\vspace{-10pt}

\[
\text{Azure resource cost} = 
3.96 \text{ €/month} \times 
\dfrac{1 \text{ month}}{30 \text{ days}} \times 
122 \text{ days} = 
\textbf{\text{€}24.16}
\]

\vspace{-5pt}

Based on the Microsoft annual fee of €99 \cite{microsoft-365-pricing}, the corresponding total cost is the following:


\vspace{-10pt}

\[
\text{Microsoft resource cost} = 
99 \text{ €/year} \times 
\dfrac{1 \text{ year}}{365 \text{ days}} \times 
122 \text{ days} = 
\textbf{\text{€}33.09}
\]

\vspace{-5pt}

Thus, the total software resources cost equals the sum of both costs:

\begin{center}
    \texteuro24.16 + \texteuro33.09 = \textbf{\text{€}57.25}
\end{center}
