\textbf{Office rental cost}

The project development always takes place in the FSP office. Therefore, this is the only cost to consider in this category. The FSP office is located in Plaça Francesc Macià, Barcelona. The monthly rental fee is €6,000. Given that there is enough space to accommodate up to 25 employees working at the same time, the total cost is calculated as follows:
    
\vspace{-25pt}

\[
\text{Office rental cost} = 
6,000 \text{ €/month} \times 
\dfrac{1 \text{ month}}{30 \text{ days}} \times 
\dfrac{1 \text{ office}}{25 \text{ employees}} \times 
122 \text{ days} = 
\textbf{\text{€}976.00}
\]
