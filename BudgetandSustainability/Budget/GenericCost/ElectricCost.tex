
\textbf{Electric cost}
\label{sec:electric-cost}
\vspace{-5pt}

Only hardware resources require electricity and are the only resources considered in this cost. Electricity costs are calculated by multiplying the current electricity cost of \textbf{0.1748 \texteuro /kWh} \cite{electricity-price-spain} by the energy consumed and the resource usage time. Table \ref{table:electricity-cost} details the calculations and the resulting total cost:
