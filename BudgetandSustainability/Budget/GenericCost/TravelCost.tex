\textbf{Travel cost}
\vspace{-5pt}

The public transport rate in Barcelona is 45.5€ per 3 months \cite{t-mobilitat-t-jove}; the total travel cost corresponds to the following equation: 

\vspace{-15pt}

\[
\text{Travel cost} = 
45.5 \text{ €/3 months} \times 
\dfrac{3 \text{ months}}{90 \text{ days}} \times 
122 \text{ days} = 
\textbf{\text{€}61.68}
\]
