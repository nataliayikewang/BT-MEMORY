\textbf{Internet cost}

The basic internet plan costs €21.95 per month \cite{orange-fiber-offer}. Since the project lasts 122 days, the internet cost is calculated as follows:

\vspace{-15pt}

\[
\text{Internet cost} = 
21.95 \text{ €/month} \times 
\dfrac{1 \text{ month}}{30 \text{ days}} \times 
122 \text{ days} = 
\textbf{\text{€}89.26}
\]
