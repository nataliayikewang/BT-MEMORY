\textbf{Office rental cost}
\vspace{-5pt}
 
The project development always takes place in the FSP office. The FSP office is located in Plaça Francesc Macià, Barcelona, and demands a monthly rental fee of €6,000. Given that there is enough space to accommodate up to 25 employees (which is the number of employees at FSP's Barcelona team, as defined in Section \ref{sec:problems-to-solve}), this is the cost calculation:

    
\vspace{-25pt}

\[
\text{Office rental cost} = 
6,000 \text{ €/month} \times 
\dfrac{1 \text{ month}}{30 \text{ days}} \times 
\dfrac{1 \text{ office}}{25 \text{ employees}} \times 
122 \text{ days} = 
\textbf{\text{€}976.00}
\]
