\subsubsection{Product Owner}

The Product Owner is responsible for understanding the client's interests and communicating them effectively to the application development team. Their principal role is to maximize the value of the final product from the client's point of view. They define the product's vision and requirements, aligning the application's features with the client's interests throughout the development lifecycle.
 
In this case, Sergio Alejandro Lobig Machado serves as Product Owner, as he is the only representative of FSP in this project.
