\subsection{Proposed Solution}
\label{sec:proposed-solution}

FSP clearly lacks a more intelligent, automated expense management strategy to prevent employee frustration and guarantee accurate reimbursement of business expenses. This project aims to develop an automated web application that allows employees to manage, report, and submit expenses seamlessly on a single platform. 


This application streamlines the entire workflow, offering effortless receipt uploads, AI-automated data extraction and efficient verification and approval processes. The primary goal is to simplify the previous expenses management workflow, optimizing time and effort for all parties,  reducing human error, and ensuring information is transmitted securely and efficiently to the appropriate approver.
