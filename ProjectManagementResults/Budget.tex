\input{ProjectManagementResults/Budget/PersonnelCostTable.tex}

By exceeding the initial total project hours, the initial estimation of generic costs is adjusted to reflect the actual project cost presented in Table \ref{tab:actual-total-generic-cost}. Only costs derived from execution hours are recalculated, namely, amortization and electricity, but their deviation is minimal. The remaining costs are based on the total project days, which coincide with the planned days; consequently, no deviation is shown from the initial calculation.
